A one-line office joke for the Make.fun 24-hour sprint bounty.

> Our Slack has 47 channels; the only one anyone reads is #random, where the CEO posts cat memes at 2 a.m. and HR pretends not to see them.

The joke avoids the crowded field angles (meetings, printers, email, HR, the fridge) and instead picks a fresh, specific scene: a Slack workspace with 47 channels, a CEO posting cat memes at 2 a.m. in #random, and HR pretending not to see them. One sentence, one clear punchline, readable at first glance.

Run `main.py` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; it writes the entry to `entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
